## Day 1 — Wave Analysis Summary

### What I saw
- Beautiful wave. She actually did the compression-extension cycle correctly — entered the wall, found speed in the highest-energy zone.
- Then the section closed on her — and she kept fighting it instead of choosing an exit.
- Two options I gave her for a closing section: (a) escape with speed (just exit the wave, not a wipeout), or (b) go UP and hit it for an off-the-lip.
- She hasn't tried off-the-lip yet — and one of these waves was perfect for it.
- A cutback in that situation = "the wave is closing on me, I'll bail AND continue with one last long turn."
- Foot work: she's bringing the back foot up first, then the front. We need to FLIP this — front foot first, then back. This is years of muscle memory to undo.
- Mid-trance pattern: she sees the wave, wants it badly, locks in narrow focus and attacks. Misses the "this is about to close, better go up-and-down" read.
- Compression note: when she's overly bent and tense, she can't move — she needs to relax first, move her feet, THEN attack.

### What's working
- The compression-extension is THERE on the entry — she found the energy zone.
- Strong drive on the wave.
- Wants it — high engagement.

### What's blocking progression
- Old foot-work pattern (back-then-front) blocking new maneuvers.
- Tunnel-vision: fighting closeouts instead of reading and exiting.
- Tension shutting down her movement options mid-wave.
- Hasn't unlocked off-the-lip or cutback yet — but the wave is offering them.

## Roadmap — What to Focus On

**Priority 1 — Flip the foot order: FRONT foot first, then back.**
- This is the unlock for everything else. She's been moving back-then-front for years.
- Drill: **minimum 10 minutes EVERY session, on land, before paddling out. Non-stop reps. No skipping.** That's the only way muscle memory rewires.
- Cue: "Front first."

**Priority 2 — Read the closeout, choose an exit.**
- When the section is closing: don't fight. Three real options:
  - Escape with speed straight off the back.
  - Go UP and hit it (off-the-lip).
  - Cutback back to the foam.
- Cue: "Stop fighting — choose a door."

**Priority 3 — First off-the-lip attempt.**
- The waves are giving her perfect setups. She's ready to try.
- Frame it as: "When you'd otherwise bail, send it up instead."

**Priority 4 — Relax to access movement.**
- When she's tense and over-bent, she has no movement options.
- Cue: "Loose feet, soft hands — THEN attack."
- Mid-length board has 3 stance positions (forward / mid / back) — needs to play between them, can't do that locked up.

**Priority 5 — Pull back from tunnel vision.**
- She's all-in on whatever she's chasing. Doesn't see the situation change.
- Cue: "One breath out, then go."
- Decision-making improves the moment she stops attacking and starts observing.

### Working hypothesis on level
Intermediate with strong drive and a years-old motor pattern in the way. The foot-order rewire is the single highest-leverage thing she can do this trip — everything downstream depends on it.
