Angola Surf Retreat — Preparation Guide

Welcome to the Angola retreat.
This guide is here to help you arrive relaxed, prepared, and ready to enjoy the experience fully.

The goal is simple:
- Don't forget important things
- Travel lighter and smarter
- Reduce unnecessary stress
- Be ready for progression, recovery, and adventure

The waves are not going anywhere.
Give yourself time to settle in, be present, and enjoy the process.

---

The Ocean Athlete Philosophy

Our philosophy is:
Minimalism • Accuracy • Preparedness

You do not need to bring everything.
You need to bring the RIGHT things.

Most of your days will revolve around:
- Surfing
- Recovery
- Food
- Connection
- Video analysis
- Rest

Pack lighter than you think.

---

Surf Equipment

Recommended Boards
- 2 surfboards
- Ideally: one main board, one backup / alternative board

Common mistake:
Bringing boards that are too performance-focused or not versatile enough for conditions.

Essential Surf Gear
- 2 sets of backup fins
- Wax
- Leash + backup leash
- Board bag
- Earplugs (if needed)
- Ding repair kit (recommended)

---

Wetsuits & Weather

Angola can feel warm during the day and chilly during mornings, evenings, wind, or long sessions.

Recommended:
- Spring suit
- Full wetsuit / long suit
- Light warm layer / hoodie
- Hat or cap

Many people regret not bringing:
- Enough wetsuit variety
- A hat
- Warm clothes for mornings/evenings

---

Clothing

You will realistically use:
- 3–4 outfits
- Swimwear most of the day
- Comfortable beach clothes

Laundry is available and usually takes around 2 days.

Common mistake:
Overpacking because of uncertainty.

You will spend most of the retreat barefoot, surfing, recovering, eating, and relaxing.
Keep it simple.

---

Video Analysis & Technology

Video coaching is a central part of the retreat experience.

To make the process smooth, please bring:

Required:
- SSD with USB-C / Type-C connection
- Laptop (strongly recommended)
- Phone chargers
- Personal charging adapter

Many students regret:
- Not bringing a laptop
- Not bringing an SSD
- Trying to store heavy video files only on their phone

Having your own setup makes reviewing footage easier, faster, and more effective.

---

Recovery & Performance

The retreat includes a high amount of surfing and physical output.

Recommended:
- Hydration bottle
- Electrolytes
- Magnesium
- Sunscreen
- Massage ball

Most students underestimate:
- Fatigue
- Recovery needs
- Sun exposure

Pace yourself.
Leave energy for the entire retreat — not only the first sessions.

Mindset & Preparation

The biggest thing that hurts progression is:
- Too many expectations
- Overthinking
- Trying to force results
- Not being present

The best students:
- Relax into the process
- Stay open-minded
- Trust the rhythm of the retreat
- Allow themselves time to settle in

Progress happens faster when you stop trying to control every outcome.

---

First 24 Hours

The first day is still travel.

There is no rush.

Our goal for the first 24 hours:
- Arrive safely
- Eat well
- Hydrate
- Settle into the compound
- Relax
- Sleep properly
- Ease into the environment

The waves are not going anywhere.

Usually after 2–3 days:
- the body settles
- the mind slows down
- the rhythm begins

Being relaxed is one of the best things you can do for your surfing and your experience.
