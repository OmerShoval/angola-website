Measurable Progress

Six things tracked every session

Progress is a feedback loop. These six metrics close the loop after every wave — because in order for something to change, something needs to change.

01
Attempt Count
How many intentional attempts were made each session. Volume of effort is the raw material of improvement — you can't refine something you won't try.
The foundation
02
Attempt Awareness
Understand. Believe. Try. Not just doing — but knowing what you're doing. Self-feedback after each attempt builds the internal coach that stays with you forever.
Self-coaching
03
Wave Length
Time on the wave matters. Longer rides mean better reading, better positioning, and more joy in the water. Have fun and enjoy — the best feeling ever.
Flow and fun
04
Wave Understanding Before
Reading the wave before committing. This is where independence is born — the ability to choose when and where without needing to be told.
Decision making
05
Wave Understanding After
What did the wave actually do? Reflecting after each wave builds pattern recognition and the ability to make better decisions on the very next one.
Pattern recognition
06
Execution Quality
Compare each attempt to the previous one — not to some abstract ideal. Honest, grounded, and focused on the actual direction of movement over time.
Refinement

Lineup Ethics

How we share the waves

The goal is to enjoy — and to keep it that way for everyone. That requires each person looking out for each other, always.

01
Noticing each other and properly taking turns is a must. The lineup is shared — act like it.
02
If you missed a wave, go back to the end of the line. Every wave is a rotation, not a race.
03
Never take a wave if it means risking someone. No wave is worth someone else's safety. Ever.
04
When coming up from a wipe-out, go wide — or go to the whitewater. Work hard to give way to the surfer riding behind you. This is the rule that shows who you are in the water.
From Your Coach

Please don't get offended

A heads-up on how I coach — and why it comes from exactly one place.

If I speak loudly — I am passionate about you succeeding. If I see someone who can do more, I will be direct. Loudness is care, not criticism.
If I get frustrated at you for not pushing it, or for forgetting something we worked on — it's because I care. I remember your sessions because they matter to me.
If I push you outside your comfort zone constantly — it's because I care. The edge of your comfort zone is exactly where progress lives.
I promise to be balanced.
The Journey

9 days.
9 distinct themes.

Each day has a single focus — a lens through which everything that session is filtered. The progression is deliberate.

Session Goals

Day 1
Arrival
Settle in, tune in
  • Late arrival, dinner, get comfortable in the space
  • Understand the coaching process — lecture in NotebookLM
  • Personal talks: what matters to you as a surfer
  • First surf — just feel the ocean, no agenda
  • Where are you now vs. where do you want to be?
Day 2
Commitment
Choosing intentional change
  • Adjustments aligned to what you care about and what will improve you most
  • Not forcing change — inviting experimentation
  • Building your personal visualization
Day 3
Wave Reading
Understanding the ocean
  • High tide vs. low tide — how it changes everything
  • Swell direction and what it means for your spot
  • Shapes that are consistently good
  • Blind spots only the locals know
  • Sunset yoga
🧘 Sunset yoga session
Day 4
Visible Shifts
Seeing yourself clearly
  • Identifying visible shifts in movement patterns
  • Setting new goals based on what's emerging
  • Working actively toward them in the water
Day 5
Microscope
Precision per person
  • Deep-dive coaching on each person's specific goal
  • Surfboard lecture — visual and conceptual
  • Evening: technical NotebookLM session
Day 6
Play
Freedom without pressure
  • Play with what we've built — just enjoy
  • Light, on-demand feedback only
  • No proving. No performance.
Day 7
The Big Questions
What comes after Angola?
  • How do we continue this in Israel?
  • What needs to be done — practically, specifically
  • Building your post-clinic plan together
Day 8
Pushing the Envelope
More. Better. Yours.
  • Trying bigger waves, better decisions, full ownership
  • Delayed feedback — given on the beach after the session
  • Independent decision-making throughout
🏖️ Beach debrief after the session
Day 9
Presence
Just surfing
  • Presence. Allowing it to be.
  • Enjoying the skills we built
  • No proving. No performance.
  • Personal progress film screening
  • Sharing circle — what changed, what stays
