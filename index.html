Measurable Progress

Progress isn't a feeling. These three metrics give you something concrete to hold onto as the days build.

01
Wave Count
How many quality waves were caught each session. A direct measure of positioning, commitment, and consistency over time.
Consistency & positioning
02
Attempt Rate
How often you intentionally tried the movement or adjustment being worked on. Tracks courage, willingness to experiment, and repetition.
Courage & repetition
03
Execution Quality
A 1–10 score for the specific technical focus of the day. Honest, precise, and focused on refinement — not just effort.
Refinement & precision

The Journey

10 days.
10 distinct themes.

Each day is built around a single theme — a lens through which everything that session is filtered. The progression is deliberate.

Progression Arc
Days 1–3: Arrival, Commitment, Rhythm
Days 4–6: Feedback, Precision, Play
Days 7–8: Recalibration, Challenge
Days 9–10: Ownership, Presence
Day 1
Arrival
Theme: Awareness
  • Spot orientation and baseline filming
  • Equipment review and first surf observation
  • Open space to settle into the environment
Day 2
Commitment
Theme: Choosing discomfort
  • Intentional adjustments — stance, timing, positioning
  • Subtle equipment refinements
  • Not forcing change — inviting experimentation
Day 3
Rhythm
Theme: Synchronization
  • Wave-reading: types, tide shifts, positioning windows
  • Consistency patterns and blind spots
  • Sunset Mobility + Breath session
🌅 Recovery: Sunset mobility session
Day 4
Feedback
Theme: Seeing clearly
  • Video comparison analysis
  • Identifying visible shifts and hidden growth
  • Open space to process individually before sharing
Day 5
Precision
Theme: Small details
  • Microscope coaching: weight, timing, body line
  • Angola progression lecture — year-to-year refinement
  • Equipment Tuning + Technical Reset workshop
🔧 Recovery: Equipment tuning workshop
Day 6
Play
Theme: Freedom
  • Very light coaching prompts only
  • Follow instinct — repeat, experiment, cruise
  • Stories of your best playful sessions
⏰ Recovery: No-alarm morning
Day 7
Recalibration
Theme: Expression
  • Board evolution lecture: longboard, shortboard, mid-length
  • Reassessment of equipment, style and goals
  • Movement expression exploration
Day 8
Challenge
Theme: Independent decision-making
  • Students choose: spot, line, tactical decisions
  • Feedback is delayed — no immediate correction
  • Solo Beach Walk Reflection after session
  • Student footage share — strengths and lessons
🚶 Recovery: Solo beach walk
Day 9
Ownership
Theme: Self-coaching
  • Students refine independently with minimal intervention
  • Only precise coaching moments when truly needed
  • Personal future roadmap — 3-month technical focus
Day 10
Presence
Theme: Just be
  • No performance. No proving. Just surfing.
  • Open surf — pure presence in the ocean
  • Personal Progress Film — Day 1 to Day 10
  • Final circle: sharing what changed
🎬 Final: Personal progress film
