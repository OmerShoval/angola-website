Surf Equipment

What to bring in the water

Focused and functional. Everything on this list has a purpose.

🏄
Recommended Boards
  • 2 surfboards total
  • One main board
  • One backup / alternative board
Common mistake: Boards too performance-focused or not versatile enough for conditions.
🔧
Essential Surf Gear
  • 2 sets of backup fins
  • Wax
  • Leash + backup leash
  • Board bag
  • Earplugs (if needed)
  • Ding-Aid repair kit — see below
🧥
Wetsuits & Weather
  • Spring suit
  • Full wetsuit / long suit
  • Light warm layer / hoodie
  • Hat or cap
Angola can feel warm during the day and chilly during mornings, evenings, and long sessions.
👕
Clothing
  • 3–4 outfits total
  • Swimwear most of the day
  • Comfortable beach clothes
  • Laundry available (~2 day turnaround)
You will spend most of the retreat barefoot. Keep it simple.
💻
Video & Technology
  • SSD with USB-C / Type-C connection
  • Laptop (strongly recommended)
  • Phone chargers
  • Personal charging adapter
Many students regret not bringing a laptop or SSD. Video coaching is central to the experience.
💊
Recovery & Performance
  • Hydration bottle
  • Electrolytes
  • Magnesium
  • Sunscreen
  • Massage ball
  • Yoga mat (beach stretching & recovery)
Most students underestimate fatigue, recovery needs, and sun exposure.
Board Repair
Ding-Aid Repair Kit

A quick, simple speed repair kit for minor dings. Poly & Epoxy formula — includes clear Ding-Aid stickers and fiber-fill resin. Durable, safe, and small enough to fit in any board bag. A ding in Angola means a session out — pack one and surf every day.

Pack one — always

Logistics

Cash, flights & local essentials

Electric Outlets
Type C — European plug

Angola uses the Type C (European) socket — two round pins. Make sure your charging adapter supports this. Most universal travel adapters will work. Voltage is 220V.

Don't forget your adapter

Cash & Local Spending

  • Personal spending varies by individual
  • ~$50 extra recommended for tips
  • ~$20 extra for a local SIM card and internet access

Flights & Board Travel

  • Longboards: Emirates or FlyDubai are usually best
  • Other boards: Most airlines work fine for standard board bags
  • We will help guide you through surfboard logistics

Coffee & Morning Essentials

  • Personal water boiler / kettle
  • Your preferred coffee setup
Many students regret not bringing these

Morning routines matter here. Simple comforts make a big difference when you're away for 10 days.

Remember

  • You will spend most days barefoot
  • Laundry is available, ~2 day turnaround
  • Overpacking creates stress, not comfort
  • The retreat provides everything else you need
From Past Students

Important points to know

Real tips from students who've been through this — arrival, logistics, and daily life at the retreat.

After Landing
1
Immigration
Go to the "no visa" line. Even if the clerk asks where your visa is, explain that we don't need one.
2
Luggage
Suitcases arrive on two separate carousels. Surfboards come out on a separate oversized baggage carousel. It can take up to an hour and a half — be patient.
3
Driver
At the exit, the driver will be waiting with a "Carpe Diem" sign.
4
SIM Card
Get a SIM card at the airport with the largest data package available — it runs out after a few days and you cannot always rely on hotel Wi-Fi. Best option: the Unitel stand. The local rep does it quickly and professionally.
5
Currency Exchange
Exchange money at the airport. There is no point in exchanging more than €50.
6
Travel Time
The drive from the airport to the hotel can take three hours. Some of the roads aren't great — take a deep breath.
Packing & Daily Tips
7
Footwear
Bring two pairs of flip-flops.
8
Clothing
Bring one pair of long pants and a sweatshirt.
9
Brushing Teeth
Do not rinse your mouth with tap water from the sink.
10
Water
Always take two bottles of water from the dining room to keep in your room.
11
Electricity
Power upstairs (eco area) usually doesn't work from late morning until 17:00. Plan accordingly.
12
Day Bag
Bring a backpack or side bag large enough to hold sunscreen, surf wax, poncho, and hat. You bring it down on the first morning and leave it downstairs. Very convenient.
13
Snacks & Treats
Bring personal treats you love — special coffee, a specific cookie, or nuts for snacking.
14
First Aid
Bring anti-chafing cream or ointment.
15
Ping Pong
A ping pong set is really important. Whoever can bring one would be greatly appreciated!
16
Mindset
"Africa Time" — take a deep breath. The pace of life is relaxed here.
