Surf Equipment

What to bring
in the water

Focused and functional. Everything on this list has a purpose.

🏄
Recommended Boards
  • 2 surfboards total
  • One main board
  • One backup / alternative board
Common mistake: Boards too performance-focused or not versatile enough for conditions.
🔧
Essential Surf Gear
  • 2 sets of backup fins
  • Wax
  • Leash + backup leash
  • Board bag
  • Earplugs (if needed)
  • Ding repair kit (recommended)
🧥
Wetsuits & Weather
  • Spring suit
  • Full wetsuit / long suit
  • Light warm layer / hoodie
  • Hat or cap
Angola can feel warm during the day and chilly during mornings, evenings, and long sessions.
👕
Clothing
  • 3–4 outfits total
  • Two pairs of flip-flops
  • One pair of long pants
  • Sweatshirt or warm layer
  • Swimwear most of the day
  • Comfortable beach clothes
  • Laundry available (~2 day turnaround)
You will spend most of the retreat barefoot. Keep it simple.
💻
Video & Technology
  • SSD with USB-C / Type-C connection
  • Laptop (strongly recommended)
  • Phone chargers
  • Personal charging adapter
Many students regret not bringing a laptop or SSD. Video coaching is central to the experience.
💊
Recovery & Performance
  • Hydration bottle
  • Electrolytes
  • Magnesium
  • Sunscreen
  • Massage ball
  • Anti-chafing cream
Most students underestimate fatigue, recovery needs, and sun exposure.

Logistics

Cash, flights
& local essentials

Electric Outlets
Type C — European plug

Angola uses the Type C (European) socket — two round pins. Make sure your charging adapter supports this. Most universal travel adapters will work. Voltage is 220V.

Don't forget your adapter

Cash & Local Spending

  • Personal spending varies by individual
  • ~$50 extra recommended for tips
  • Exchange money at the airport — no point changing more than 50 Euros
  • Get a local SIM at the airport: go to the Unitel stand and ask for the largest data package. The data runs out after a few days and hotel Wi-Fi is unreliable — ~$20

Flights & Board Travel

  • Longboards: Emirates or FlyDubai are usually best
  • Other boards: Most airlines work fine for standard board bags
  • We will help guide you through surfboard logistics

Coffee & Morning Essentials

  • Personal water boiler / kettle
  • Your preferred coffee setup
Many students regret not bringing these

Morning routines matter here. Simple comforts make a big difference when you're away for 10 days.

Remember

  • You will spend most days barefoot
  • Laundry is available, ~2 day turnaround
  • Overpacking creates stress, not comfort
  • The retreat provides everything else you need
At the Airport

After you land —
step by step.

What to expect from the moment you touch down in Luanda.

🛂
Immigration
Go to the "no visa" line. If the officer asks about your visa, calmly explain that you don't need one.
🧳
Luggage
Bags arrive on two separate carousels. Surfboards are on a separate oversized baggage carousel. Getting out can take up to 1.5 hours — be patient.
🪧
Your Pick-up
At the exit, your driver will be waiting with a "Carpe Diem" sign.
📱
SIM Card
Get a local SIM at the Unitel stand — ask for the largest data package. The data runs out after a few days and hotel Wi-Fi is unreliable.
💶
Currency
Exchange money at the airport. There's no point exchanging more than 50 Euros.
🚗
The Drive
Airport to the hotel can take up to 3 hours. Some roads aren't great. Take a breath, relax, and enjoy the scenery.
"Africa Time" — patience is part of the journey. The pace here is relaxed, and that's exactly the point.
Tips from Past Participants

Life at
the retreat

Small things that make a big difference, straight from people who've been there.

🚰
Tap Water

When brushing your teeth, do not rinse your mouth with tap water from the sink. Use bottled water.

💧
Drinking Water

Always take two bottles of water from the dining room to keep in your room. Staying hydrated between sessions matters more than you'd expect.

⚡
Electricity

Power in the upstairs eco sleeping area usually doesn't work from late morning until 5:00 PM. Plan your charging around this.

🎒
Day Bag

Bring a large, comfortable backpack or shoulder bag for your sunscreen, wax, poncho, and hat. On the first morning you bring it down — and it stays downstairs for the week. Very convenient.

🍫
Snacks & Personal Treats

Pack whatever you love to snack on — your own coffee, a favourite type of biscuit, nuts, seeds, chocolate. Small comforts go a long way on a 10-day trip.

🏓
Ping Pong Set

A ping pong set is hugely popular at the retreat. If someone in the group can bring one, it would be very much appreciated.
