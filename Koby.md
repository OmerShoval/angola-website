## Roadmap — What to Focus On

**Priority 1 — Pop-up workaround (mental + physical).**
- Known physical limitation we work on long-term. Don't pretend it's not there.
- Strategy on critical sections: pull harder, twist head sideways, push more with hand pressure. Even drop half-wave on the belly to get through the section, THEN stabilize.
- "Pros do this. It will multiply your wave count."
- Cue: "Survive the section, then ride."

**Priority 2 — Address the fear loop directly.**
- Land conversation: "You don't fear wipeouts. So what are you actually afraid of?" Let him sit with it.
- In water: be his external voice. "Go." "Take it." "Don't hesitate." He asked for this.

**Priority 3 — Stay in the upper portion of the wall.**
- He drops to the bottom and loses speed. Stay middle/top.
- Cue: "Don't dig to the bottom — climb from the middle."

**Priority 4 — Soft entry angle.**
- Approach the wall at a gentle angle, then climb. Not at the beach.
- Cue: "Go ALONG the wall, not AT the beach."

**Priority 5 — Compress + extend, not just press.**
- Knee bend → body extension up (chest rises, hands lift).
- He's currently just pressing the toes. Energy trapped down.

**Priority 6 — Soften the turns.**
- Less hard turning. Softer pressure changes so he stays connected to the wall instead of bouncing off it.

**Priority 7 — Weight distribution: back straight, knees work.**
- He thinks he needs to go LOWER. He doesn't. Shoulders open + timing.
- Cue: "Tall back, busy knees."

### Working hypothesis on level
Beginner-intermediate stuck on a real physical block AND a fear loop. The pop-up workaround unlocks wave count; the fear conversation unlocks committed waves. Both have to happen.
