## 🎯 How to Approach Tali — Read This First

**She's coachable, intuitive, and articulate about what she wants.** Her core word is **flow** — that's her north star. Frame everything around it.

- **Style mix:** she's "fire" — intense. She likes a structured ladder ("give me one point, I work it, then the next") AND needs you to occasionally push her with a "Hello, GO!" when she's holding back. Both/and.
- **She does the right things intuitively** but has never thought about them consciously. Your job is to bring **intention** to what she already does by feel. The minute she has a conscious cue, she sees the difference.
- **She likes refinement over flash.** If forced to choose between cross-stepping reps and cleaner flow, she'll pick flow. Honor that — but still introduce cross-stepping in parallel because she said both matter to her.
- **Two boards in play** — her CJ Nelson (longer, step-style, requires foot movement) and her Pyzel (more dynamic). At home she rides the CJ more. Plan: choose the board to match the day's teaching goal, not just the conditions.
- **She likes the edge** — "a little scared and succeeding" is her sweet spot. The bigger sets coming from far ("monsters") scare her, especially on the inside where the wave is stronger and steeper. She defaults wider/outside. Push her toward the inside gradually.

## Day 1 — Wave Analysis Summary

### What I saw
- Beautiful wave choice, reading the wall well.
- Up-and-down flow on the face is clean — she gets the movement intuitively.
- Bottom-to-top transition is there, but the energy isn't being released — she's compressing at the bottom without pushing through extension on the way up.
- Back-side hand keeps drifting back behind her hip and pulling her off balance.
- When she wants to reconnect to the whitewater, she's turning without re-positioning her feet first — it kills her flow back.

### What's working
- Intuition for the wave — she knows where to go.
- The "I want to come back to it" instinct kicked in (great wave awareness).
- Clean entry, soft pop-up.

### What's blocking progression
- Compression without extension = no upward release of energy.
- Hand discipline (back hand crossing the midline of the hip).
- Foot position not adjusted before the turn back to the foam.

## Roadmap — What to Focus On

**Priority 1 — Compress like a spring, then extend.** (serves her flow goal directly)
- Cue: "Knees bend at the bottom → push the chest up at the top."
- Drill: 5 turns where she calls out "down" and "up" so the rhythm becomes conscious.

**Priority 2 — Hand stays connected to the hip on back-side.**
- Cue: "Imaginary line down your hip — back hand never crosses it."
- Visual that landed: "Someone pulling your hand back = falling backward."

**Priority 3 — Feet move BEFORE the turn, not during.**
- Sequence: back foot adjust → front foot adjust → THEN initiate the turn back to foam.
- Cue: "Don't turn until your feet are set."

**Priority 4 — Bring intention to the intuitive.**
- She's doing the right things by feel, loses them under pressure. Build conscious checkpoints.

**Priority 5 — Cross-step drill on the CJ.**
- She specifically asked for this. Start dry-land on her glider, then water.
- Frame it as a 1–2 year body-rewiring project that "destroys flow at first then locks in beautifully."

**Priority 6 — Inside-section confidence (stretch goal).**
- Gradually move her closer to inside on smaller sets, then larger. Use her "scared + succeeding" sweet spot.
